\begin{cabstract}
近年来，随着智能化出行技术的发展，面向园区、景区、社区等封闭场景的低速载人代步装备受到广泛关注。此类场景具有道路形式多样、行人干扰频繁、安全要求较高等特点，对车辆运行稳定性、环境感知能力以及自主通行能力提出更高要求。

传统两轮平衡车多依赖倒立摆原理实现动态平衡，难以兼顾结构稳定性和乘坐舒适性；基于双目视觉的障碍物感知方法已由传统几何方法发展到深度学习驱动的双目匹配与目标识别融合，但在嵌入式平台上仍面临感知精度与实时性难以统一的问题；自主行驶与避障研究则不断向动态障碍处理和安全决策优化发展，但在复杂动态环境下的可靠避障能力仍有待提升。

本文围绕两轮平衡车开展总体设计。在机械结构方面，采用对称双控制力矩陀螺布置与车轮倒立摆控制相结合的载人平衡方案，并对关键部件进行强度校核与有限元分析；在控制系统方面，以STM32F407单片机和FreeRTOS软件框架为基础，进行硬件线路设计与搭建软件环境。

在环境感知方面围绕双目视觉感知开展研究。立体匹配部分提出轻量化HAGVNet模型用于视差估计，其中分层注意力引导代价体用于引入粗尺度几何先验并增强细尺度代价体构建过程中的几何一致性，采用ConvNeXtV2风格二维代价聚合模块提升代价聚合阶段的特征表达能力；目标检测部分采用YOLO11n模型识别典型障碍物，并结合深度图信息实现障碍物检测、测距与空间定位，同时完成立体匹配与目标检测模型的TensorRT加速部署。

基于ROS构建两轮平衡车自主导航系统。定位部分采用卡尔曼滤波融合GPS、IMU和轮速计信息进行位姿估计；导航部分采用Dijkstra算法进行全局路径规划，采用TEB算法进行局部轨迹优化与动态避障；感知部分基于人工势场中斥力场的方法，将障碍物列表映射至局部代价地图并参与路径规划。

通过基本行驶性能、双目视觉环境感知和闭环避障实验，对两轮平衡车系统进行验证。实验结果表明，所设计两轮平衡车具备基本行驶、环境感知与自主避障能力，实现了感知、规划与控制闭环运行。
\end{cabstract}

\begin{eabstract}
In recent years, with the development of intelligent mobility technologies, low-speed passenger-carrying vehicles for enclosed scenarios such as parks, scenic areas, and communities have attracted increasing attention. These scenarios are characterized by diverse road forms, frequent pedestrian interference, and high safety requirements, which place higher demands on vehicle stability, environmental perception, and autonomous traversal capability.

Traditional two-wheeled self-balancing vehicles mainly rely on the inverted-pendulum principle to maintain dynamic balance, making it difficult to simultaneously achieve structural stability and ride comfort. Stereo-vision-based obstacle perception has evolved from traditional geometric methods to deep-learning-based fusion of stereo matching and object recognition, but balancing perception accuracy and real-time performance on embedded platforms remains challenging. Research on autonomous driving and obstacle avoidance is also advancing toward dynamic-obstacle handling and safety-oriented decision optimization, yet reliable obstacle avoidance in complex dynamic environments still needs further improvement.

This thesis presents the overall design of a two-wheeled self-balancing vehicle. In terms of mechanical structure, a passenger-carrying balancing scheme combining a symmetric dual control moment gyroscope configuration with wheel inverted-pendulum control is adopted, and strength verification and finite-element analysis are carried out for key components. In terms of the control system, hardware circuit design and software environment construction are completed based on the STM32F407 microcontroller and the FreeRTOS software framework.

For environmental perception, research is conducted on stereo-vision-based perception. For stereo matching, a lightweight HAGVNet model is proposed for disparity estimation. In this model, the hierarchical attention-guided cost volume introduces coarse-scale geometric priors and enhances the geometric consistency of fine-scale cost volume construction, while the ConvNeXtV2-style 2D cost aggregation module improves feature representation during cost aggregation. For object detection, the YOLO11n model is used to identify typical obstacles. By combining detection results with depth information, the system realizes obstacle detection, ranging, and spatial localization, and TensorRT-accelerated deployment is completed for both the stereo matching and object detection models.

A ROS-based autonomous navigation system is constructed for the two-wheeled self-balancing vehicle. In the localization module, GPS, IMU, and wheel-speed information are fused through Kalman filtering for pose estimation. In the navigation module, the Dijkstra algorithm is used for global path planning, and the TEB algorithm is adopted for local trajectory optimization and dynamic obstacle avoidance. In the perception-related part, obstacle lists are mapped to the local costmap based on the repulsive field in the artificial potential field method and then incorporated into path planning.

The system is validated through experiments on basic driving performance, stereo-vision-based environmental perception, and closed-loop obstacle avoidance. The results show that the designed two-wheeled self-balancing vehicle possesses basic driving, environmental perception, and autonomous obstacle-avoidance capabilities, realizing closed-loop operation of perception, planning, and control.
\end{eabstract}
